GRC / Security Awareness

Her Legacy Cybersecurity Awareness Training Manual

Timothy Dada · Independent security awareness project · 2025

Objective

Her Legacy is a nonprofit supporting women, families, and community programs. I built a full cybersecurity awareness training manual for their staff and volunteers — scoped to the specific risks a donor-facing, participant-data-handling nonprofit actually faces, rather than a generic security-101 handout.

Approach

The manual is organized around Her Legacy's actual data: donor financial information, volunteer and participant records, and information relating to children and families. Each of those three categories gets its own dedicated section with specific handling guidance, instead of being folded into one generic "protect sensitive data" slide.

What's Inside

  • Why Cybersecurity Matters & Understanding Her Legacy's Risks — phishing targeting donation campaigns, fraudulent donor-information requests, weak passwords, unauthorized access to participant records, ransomware, social engineering, and AI misuse.
  • Phishing Awareness — warning signs, a hover-before-you-click habit, and a worked scenario: an email claiming to be from a donor requesting an urgent payment-detail change, verified through a separate channel before acting.
  • Password Security & MFA — passphrases over passwords, no reuse across accounts, approved password managers.
  • Donor Security — access restricted to authorized personnel, payment requests verified out-of-band, no donor records over unsecured channels.
  • Volunteer & Participant Data Protection — access limited to legitimate business need; no storage on personal devices without authorization.
  • Children's Privacy & Data Protection — extra care for information relating to minors, consent requirements, and minimizing unnecessary sharing of participant photos, documents, and contact information.
  • Safe Use of Artificial Intelligence — acceptable vs. unacceptable uses of tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, and Claude; a verification checklist before trusting AI output; and deepfake awareness for AI-generated voice/video impersonation attempts.
  • Incident Reporting Process — a four-step Stop / Disconnect / Report / Document sequence, written so a non-technical staff member can follow it under pressure.
  • Knowledge Check — five review questions covering phishing, MFA, AI data handling, and incident escalation.
  • Recommendations — annual awareness training, MFA rollout, quarterly phishing simulations, AI usage guidelines, and periodic policy review.

Outcome

Delivered as a standalone training manual for internal use by Her Legacy staff and volunteers, written for both self-paced reading and instructor-led sessions.
